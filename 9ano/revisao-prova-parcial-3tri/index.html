1 · GRAMMAR — Quantifiers with or without ofSB p. 71

Quantifiers tell us how many or how much. They come before a noun.

Os quantifiers indicam quantidade. Vêm antes do substantivo.
QuantifierAmountExample
all100%All of the students love music.
most~90%Most players attend practice every week.
many / a lot of~60%Many of the girls speak two languages.
some~40%Some students want to be professional players.
a few~10%A few girls were born in another country.
none0%None of our parents want to wash our uniform.
RULE 1 — Use of when there is a determiner
Use quantifier + of when a determiner (the, my, your, our, this, these, those) comes before the noun.
Use of quando houver um determinante antes do substantivo.
  • All of the children speak English.
  • Many of my friends live abroad.
  • Most of these players are professional.
RULE 2 — No of when we speak in general
Use the quantifier alone when you talk about people or things in general.
Sem of quando falamos de pessoas ou coisas em geral.
  • All cats love milk.
  • Most players attend practice.
  • Many people love animals.
RULE 3 — none always takes of
We cannot use of with no. We must use none of + determiner.
  • ✓ None of the students speak Japanese.
  • ✗ No of the students speak Japanese.
COMMON ERRORS
  • ✗ None students speak Japanese. → ✓ None of the students speak Japanese.
  • ✗ All the cats love milk. (gatos em geral) → ✓ All cats love milk.
TESTE RÁPIDO: tem the / my / these depois do quantifier? → usa of. Não tem? → sem of.

4 · SPEAKING — Making polite suggestionsSB p. 84

Usamos estas estruturas para sugerir uma ideia de forma educada — não como ordem.

StructureExample
How about + verb-ing?How about traveling together?
What about + verb-ing?What about going in July?
Why don't we + base verb?Why don't we make a vlog about the trip?
Shall we + base verb?Shall we get jobs there?
We could + base verbWe could travel to all four countries.
Maybe we should + base verbMaybe we should go after we graduate.
Let's + base verbLet's stay together.
If you + past, you could…If you went to South Africa, you could go on a safari!
Accepting
  • That's a great idea!
  • Sounds good to me.
  • Sure, let's do that.
Disagreeing politely
  • I'm not sure about that.
  • I'd prefer to travel in July.
  • That sounds nice, but…
ATENÇÃO!
✗ How about to travel together? → ✓ How about traveling together?
✗ Why don't we going to Italy? → ✓ Why don't we go to Italy?
